Beyond Bessel's Correction: Power-Scale Concavity-Convexity Duality, Kurtosis-Optimal Shrinkage (KPS), and Empirical-Bayes GRPO

Andrew Rober Fouad Samy · BytesGlue (Dubai, United Arab Emirates) · andrew.rober@bytesglue.com
📄 Download Full Paper (PDF) 💻 GitHub Code & Benchmarks 🤗 HuggingFace Repository 🔗 LinkedIn

Abstract

Proves symbolically and empirically (on NVIDIA GB10 Grace Blackwell across Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, BGE-Large, and multi-task GRPO RLVR) that Bessel's n-1 divisor is strictly biased for every nonlinear power sigma^p (p != 2) and inadmissible in MSE on heavy-tailed neural activations. Derives the exact Kurtosis-Optimal Minimum-MSE Variance Divisor (cutting NVFP4 n=16 outlier-channel MSE by 55.4%), the Power-Scale Concavity-Convexity Duality Law, and Empirical-Bayes Group Normalization (EB-GRPO), restoring >90% coupled policy-gradient signal at small rollout group sizes G=2..4 and improving multi-task RLVR Pass@1 by +112.6% over Dr. GRPO.
